Our Story

Rooted in California.
Grown with purpose.

Early On was founded on a simple belief: the food we give our children in their earliest years shapes everything that comes after. That's why we partner exclusively with California-certified organic farms to source every ingredient — from our oats and grains to our vegetables and dairy.

We are a WIC-approved brand because we believe access to quality nutrition should never depend on a family's income. Every product we make meets the strict nutritional guidelines set by the California WIC program, so parents can shop with confidence knowing Early On has been independently reviewed and approved.

🌱

Organic from the ground up

All ingredients are USDA Certified Organic — grown without synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers.

🔬

Science-backed nutrition

Our formulations are developed in consultation with pediatric nutrition experts to meet key developmental milestones.

♻️

Sustainability first

From regenerative farming partnerships to recyclable packaging — we're committed to a healthier planet for the kids we feed.
